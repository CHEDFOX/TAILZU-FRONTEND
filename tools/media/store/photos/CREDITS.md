All from Unsplash, under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or

behind or in silhouette, a face hidden by leaves. No brand logo or other app is on show in a frame (a laptop's or a phone's
own screen is behind a card or turned away). Downloaded from images.unsplash.com at the size noted; graded in
build.mjs (cropping, light, a warm cast, vignette, grain), never retouched.

## App marks (the `tiles` frame only)

From [simple-icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) 13.21.0, CC0 1.0 (the SVG paths; the marks remain their
owners' trademarks). Each is drawn once, in its brand's own colour, on a plain white tile, to show an integration X0 Bot
really has (src/connectors in the backend: the Google Docs, Sheets and Maps, Gmail, WhatsApp, Telegram and YouTube apps and
hand-offs, Notion, and the code-hosting connection the Code bot uses). Nominative use only: no text claims a partnership or
endorsement, and the store text names no brand. Google Calendar and Google Tasks are not shown (the app has its own calendar
and to-dos).

## The mic key (Keybot frame)

X0 Bot's own art: the keyboard's Peek-a-bot mic key, drawn by `../peekbot.js`, a verbatim copy of the reference drawing
code the iOS and Android keys are ported from, held in one moment (listening) and rendered at 600 px.

## Typefaces (fonts/)

- Newsreader (Production Type), variable, SIL Open Font License 1.1: `OFL-Newsreader.txt`. The headlines.
- Geist (Vercel), SIL Open Font License 1.1: `OFL-Geist.txt`. The status bar and the feature graphic's name line.

The app's own screens use the fonts the app ships (drawn by its renderer from the backend's tulmi/fonts).
