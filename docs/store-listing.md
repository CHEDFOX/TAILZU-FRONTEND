# X0 Bot · store listing

Paste-ready text for the App Store and Google Play, for the version that turns the keyboard into a team of bots.
Every field is in a `text` block; copy the block as it is. The number in each heading is the field's length and its
limit, checked by `node tools/media/store/check-listing.mjs` (`--write` refreshes the counts after an edit). The same
script checks the store rules: no price or amount, no other company's or app's name, no "best" or ranking, nothing that
is not in the build.

Bundle id `com.tulmi.app` (both stores) · site https://x0bot.com · support support@x0bot.com

**The idea, in one line:** a team of bots, players that make your life easy, each great at one thing. The words are
written so the team can grow: nothing promises a fixed number of bots, and nothing names a bot that is not in this build.

### Add only when androidHealth.production is on

**The Play text above is the OFF variant, and it is the default**, because `androidHealth.production` in
`app/native-inputs.lock` is `off`: the release candidate is built without Health Connect unless Google has approved the
form by release-candidate day, and a listing that promises Health Connect for a binary that has none is the same mistake
as one that hides it. Play asks the listing and the in-app disclosures to represent the health access accurately either
way (Play help 12991134).

When the Android build being uploaded WAS built with Health Connect (`TULMI_ANDROID_HEALTH=on`, which the lock records
per build profile), make these three changes and run
`node tools/media/store/check-listing.mjs --android-health on --write`:

1. **Full description**, at the end of YOUR OWN BOTS, after "You can also add bots that others have made.":
   > Connect Health Connect to a fitness bot you make, so it counts your steps and workouts.
2. **Release notes**, in the last line, in place of "your own bots":
   > your own bots (a fitness one counts your workouts)
3. **App access instructions**, in place of the last sentence of the third paragraph:
   > Count my workouts: Bots › Gym Buddy › Edit › Count my workouts from Health Connect (steps and exercise only, 31 days,
   > deleted when switched off). The review account runs the space "Review Family", already has the bot "Gym Buddy", and is
   > on the allowlist that shows the Health Connect row while Google's own list is still propagating.

**Sleep has its OWN switch, and its own line** (build 8.9.1, `TULMI_ANDROID_HEALTH_SLEEP`, which is on only when BOTH it and
`TULMI_ANDROID_HEALTH` are, and only once Play has approved the refiled Health Connect form with Sleep on it). The lock
records it per profile beside `androidHealth`. Only when the build being uploaded has `READ_SLEEP`:

4. **Full description**, after the Health Connect sentence above:
   > It can count how long you sleep, too, if you turn that on.
5. **App access instructions**, after the Count my workouts sentence:
   > Also count my sleep: the sub-row under it, asked in its own Health Connect prompt, so refusing sleep leaves steps
   > working. Minutes slept per night only: no bed or wake times and no stages. The same account is on the sleep allowlist.

With the sleep switch off, neither line may be there: a listing that promises sleep for a binary with no `READ_SLEEP` is the
same mistake as one that hides it (Play help 12991134).

The App Store text is NOT conditional: the Apple Health sentence is in the Description and What's New whenever the build
carries `HEALTH_USAGE`, and the checker fails without it (App Review 2.5.1).

### The three Android halves of 8.9.1 are ON, and the Play text above says so

**`androidCalendar`, `androidMotion` and `androidArrival` are `on` for `preview` and `production`** in
`app/native-inputs.lock`, so their lines are no longer a conditional addition: they are in **App access instructions** above,
and the **Full description** has the "FROM YOUR PHONE, ONLY IF YOU TURN IT ON" paragraph and the **Release notes** the six
8.9.1 bullets. `node tools/media/store/check-listing.mjs` reads the lock and fails both ways round — a line that is there for
a build without the switch, and a switch with no line — so the two can never drift (Play help 12991134).

Each is still a separate build switch, so if Play asks for one to be turned back off, turn it off in `app/eas.json`, run
`node tools/ci/check-runtime.mjs --write`, and take that one's words back out of the three Play fields:

- **`TULMI_ANDROID_CALENDAR`** (`READ_CALENDAR`, read only, no Play declaration at all): the "Plan around my calendar"
  sentences in App access instructions, and "read when you're busy in your calendar" and "Add a plan to your calendar".
- **`TULMI_ANDROID_MOTION`** (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, no Play declaration): the "Count my steps" and "Wait while I'm
  driving" sentences, the step-counter bullet and the "in a car?" bullet.
- **`TULMI_ANDROID_ARRIVAL`** (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` + `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`): the "Remind me when I get there"
  sentences and bullet, "remind you when you reach a place", and the Full description's own background-location
  sentence ("uses location data in the background, even when the app is closed or not in use"), which Play's prominent
  disclosure asks for **in the app description** as well as in the app (Play help 9799150). `check-listing.mjs` fails
  both ways round on that sentence too.

**ARRIVAL IS THE ONE PLAY REVIEWS BY HAND, AND IT IS AN OWNER STEP, NOT A CHECK.** `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` needs Play's
**background-location declaration, a demo video and a human review**, and the Android release may not be uploaded until that
declaration is APPROVED. The "Remind me when I get there" sentences above are also the words of the declaration and the
video: the video must show that card before the prompt, and the declaration must say the same use and no other. The backend
repo's `docs/owner-8.9.1.md` has the full wording.

## Screenshots

> **THE SCREENSHOTS AND THE FRAMES STILL DRAW THE OLD BOT.** On 13 Nov the app's icon became the dotted Blip
> (`tools/media/brand/blip-icon/cool`), and `art.mjs` still paints the earlier black bot with the cream eyes from
> `tools/media/brand/x0-icon` — so re-running `build.mjs` reproduces the old character, it does not fix it. The four
> **icon files and the Play feature graphic are fixed**: `node tools/media/store/store-icons.mjs` makes them from
> `app/assets/icon.png` itself, the file the binary ships, so they can never fall behind the app again, and its
> `play-feature.png` supersedes `build.mjs`'s. Porting `art.mjs` to the Blip is its own job, and until it is done the
> phone screenshots are the one part of the Play listing that is off-brand. Listing graphics can be replaced at any
> time, with no new build and no re-review, so this does not hold up an upload.

Built by `node tools/media/store/build.mjs` (one command: it renders the app's real screens with the server's own
builders and the phone's own renderer, then composes every frame). Files are in `tools/media/store/out/`:
`ios-6.9/` (1320 x 2868), `ios-6.5/` (1284 x 2778), `play-phone/` (1080 x 1920) and `play-feature.png` (1024 x 500),
all RGB PNG without alpha, sRGB, and `search-mock.png`, the first three as App Store search shows them. Upload them in
this order. The frames are data (`tools/media/store/frames.mjs`): a new bot's frame is one entry there.

The look is the app's own: white paper, ink and greys, colour only where it is a bot's (Code moss, Keybot clay, Plan
ochre, Travel sea) and the icon's clay bar. A heavy Geist headline, left aligned, two lines with no full stop, one word
written by hand with a brush underline in the frame's bot's colour (ink when it is no one bot's). The painted black bot of
the icon is on every frame.

**The first three are one idea each, and one panorama.** App Store search shows an app's first three portrait
screenshots side by side, tiny, and Google Play shows its first ones large; so each of the three says one thing in three
or four words of big type, with one big picture, and one clay bar (the icon's) runs across all three at the same height:
from it hang the bot, the X0 widget and a phone. After them come the app's screens as white cards: the top of a real
screen, one part lifted out with an arrow at it, and the bot peeking over the card's edge with a face for the frame.

## Before you submit

- **Review account.** The app signs in with a one-time code. Make an account for review whose sign-in works without a
  live inbox, and put its details in App Store Connect (App Review Information) and Play Console (App access). The
  review sign-in only works while the server has BOTH `REVIEW_EMAIL` and `REVIEW_CODE` set (unset is the normal state
  in production), and they have to match the User Name and Password in those two forms and stay set until the app is
  approved. The notes say to type the Password where the app asks for the code, because the app's own screen calls it
  a code and a reviewer who looks for a password field finds none.
- **Fill in "Tested on"** in the App Review notes with the device and iOS version you signed in on. The notes end with
  `Tested on: [device, iOS version]`, and `check-listing.mjs` counts characters, not placeholders: pasted as it is, the
  reviewer reads the brackets. Apple's complete-review page asks for the devices and OS versions tested.
- **The AI permission comes first.** Home asks it before anything else, and most bots, dictation and Ask wait for
  Agree. The review notes say so and ask the reviewer to tap Agree; a reviewer who taps the other way finds the bots
  idle and the app looks broken (App Review 5.1.2(i)).
- **The features the notes name have to be on.** Every 8.9.1 row is behind a server switch, and most ship off. Turn
  them on for the account that reviews before submitting, and sign in on TestFlight and tap every row the notes name
  (the backend's `docs/owner-8.9.1.md` section 7 has the command). A row the notes name and the reviewer cannot
  reach is 2.1; a feature in the binary the notes never name is 2.3.1(a).
- **What's New promises sleep and the driving check.** Both are in this build, and both are behind their own server
  switch. Either widen them for everyone on release day (`PERM_HEALTH_SLEEP_IOS_USERS` emptied, `PERM_MOTION_DRIVE`
  on) or take their words out of What's New, the Description and the review notes before the build — a listing that
  offers what the switch withholds is 2.3.
- **Account deletion on the phone.** Done: You › Your data › Delete account on the phone, and You, Privacy and Settings
  on the computer (`bots.delete-account`). The review notes point there.
- **Support URL.** It is https://x0bot.com/faq, which answers today (x0bot.com/support does not). Once the server is deployed, use
  https://x0bot.com/support instead (it has support@x0bot.com on it).
- **The icon.** The painted bot hanging under the clay bar (`tools/media/brand/x0-icon/curious-hang/8-painted-clay-bar`).
  The build carries it (`app/assets/icon.png`, and the Android adaptive layers), so the App Store takes it from the
  build. Upload these by hand, from `tools/media/store/out/icons/`:
  - Google Play › Main store listing › App icon: `play-512.png` (512 x 512).
  - Google Cloud › OAuth consent screen › App logo: `google-oauth-120.png` (120 x 120). Changing the logo may ask for
    the consent screen to be verified again; keep it in production while that runs.
  - `app-store-1024.png` is the App Store's 1024 icon, if a form asks for it.
