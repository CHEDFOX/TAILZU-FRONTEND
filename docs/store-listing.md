script checks the store rules: no price or amount, no other company's or app's name, no "best" or ranking, nothing that
is not in the build.

**The idea, in one line:** a team of bots, players that make your life easy, each great at one thing. The words are
written so the team can grow: nothing promises a fixed number of bots, and nothing names a bot that is not in this build.

### Description · 3386 / 4000

```text
X0 Bot is a team of bots: players that make your life easy. Each one is great at one thing. Say what you need, by voice or by typing, in the languages you mix every day, and the right bot picks it up.

KEYBOT · WRITES FOR YOU, IN ANY APP
A voice keyboard that turns what you say into clean, ready-to-send text, wherever you type. Speak in English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Arabic or any of more than 50 languages, even two in one sentence. Filler goes, punctuation comes in, and names stay the way you said them. Keybot writes a little differently for each app: easy in chats, clear and polite in mail, short at work. Teach it your words and your style, and it keeps them.

PLAN · YOUR DAY, PLANNED
Your own calendar and to-do list, inside the app. Say "dentist Friday 5 pm" or "gym tomorrow at 7" and it is on your day. Plan shows how long it takes to get from one place to the next, tells you when to leave, and reminds you before it matters.

TRAVEL · TRIPS, SORTED
One place for a trip: the dates, the tickets, the weather on your days, stays compared by how far they are from where you will be, and a packing list. Travel finds the options and opens them for you to book. You always press pay.

NOTES · EVERY MEETING, WRITTEN UP
With X0 Bot taking notes on your computer, every meeting comes back as a summary, the decisions, and the to-dos with who owns each one. Tick them off on your phone, or add them to your own list.

CODE BOT · SHIP CODE BY ASKING
Tell it what to build or fix. Code bot reads your project, works on a safe copy, tests every change and asks when it needs you. Nothing goes live until you say yes.

SPACES · FAMILY AND FRIENDS
Make a space for home, for friends or for a team. Share lists, trips, plans and files, chat one-to-one or in groups, and give someone a task that lands on their day. You choose what each person can do, and reporting and blocking are built in.

YOUR OWN BOTS
Describe a bot in a sentence: "a bot that plans my workouts around my calendar and reminds me the night before." X0 Bot drafts it with a name, its rules and what it may use. Try it, change it, and it joins your team. You can also add bots that others have made.

ASK ANY BOT
One place to start. Type, or hold to speak, add a photo or a file, and X0 Bot hands it to the bot that fits. Activity shows what every bot did and what is waiting for you.

YOU STAY IN CHARGE
Your bots ask before they book, pay or message anyone. We never sell your data and never use it to train AI models. Download or delete your data whenever you like.

ON YOUR COMPUTER TOO
X0 Bot also runs on your computer. Sign in there by scanning a code with your phone, and your bots, notes and chats are on both.

PLANS
X0 Bot is free to start, with a monthly allowance for all your bots. Lite and Elite are monthly subscriptions that give you more every month: Lite 11 times the free allowance, Elite 150 times. The app shows each plan with its price before you buy.

Payment is charged to your App Store account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that period ends. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Terms of use: https://x0bot.com/terms
Privacy policy: https://x0bot.com/privacy
Questions: support@x0bot.com
```

### App Review notes · 2532 / 4000

```text
SIGN IN
Use the review account in App Store Connect (App Review Information). On the first screen tap "I already have an account" and sign in with it. You land on Bots (Home).

WHAT THE APP IS
X0 Bot is a team of bots, each for one kind of job. They live on Home; a tap opens one. Search (magnifier, top right) finds any bot by name, and "Ask any bot…" at the bottom sends a request to the bot that fits.

WHERE EACH BOT IS
- Keybot (voice keyboard): Home › Keybot, or Search "Keybot". To type with it in other apps: Keybot › Turn on Keybot, then Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › X0 Bot, and allow Full Access. Full Access is used only to send the voice to our server and bring the text back; it listens only while the mic key is held. A keyboard extension cannot record audio itself, so the app keeps a microphone session that the keyboard uses. While it is on, iOS shows the microphone indicator and a Live Activity, and it can be stopped from there or in the app at any time.
- Plan, Travel and Code bot: their tiles on Home, or Search by name. Code bot works on code projects you connect; "Start a new project from scratch" works without connecting anything.
- Notes: the Notes strip on Home. Meetings are noted by X0 Bot on a computer; the phone shows them.
- Spaces and chats: You › Spaces and chats.
- Make your own bot: the "Make a bot" tile on Home.
- Activity tab: what the bots did and what needs a decision.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Lite and Elite are auto-renewable monthly subscriptions (In-App Purchase, one subscription group). The purchase screen is You › Plan and billing › See plans (also reached from You › Usage when the allowance runs low). It shows each plan with the App Store's own price for it, the renewal terms, and links to the Terms of use and the Privacy policy. Without a subscription, every bot works with a smaller monthly allowance.

USER CONTENT, REPORTING AND BLOCKING
In Spaces, people share lists, files and messages with people they invite. Report and Block are on every chat (the button in a chat's header, and Report under each message), on each member's page and on each shared item. Blocked people are listed in You › Blocked people.

ACCOUNT AND DATA
Delete account: You › Your data › Delete account. Download or delete data: You › Your data.

NOTHING HAPPENS WITHOUT A YES
Bots ask before they book, pay or message anyone. Bookings open the provider's own site, and payments open the person's own payment app; X0 Bot never pays by itself.

Contact: support@x0bot.com
```

## Screenshots

Built by `node tools/media/store/build.mjs` (one command: it renders the app's real screens with the server's own
builders and the phone's own renderer, then composes every frame). Files are in `tools/media/store/out/`:
`ios-6.9/` (1320 x 2868), `ios-6.5/` (1284 x 2778), `play-phone/` (1080 x 1920) and `play-feature.png` (1024 x 500),
all RGB PNG without alpha, sRGB, and `search-mock.png`, the first three as App Store search shows them. Upload them in
this order. The frames are data (`tools/media/store/frames.mjs`): a new bot's frame is one entry there.

The look is the app's own: white paper, ink and greys, colour only where it is a bot's (Code moss, Keybot clay, Plan
ochre, Travel sea) and the icon's clay bar. A heavy Geist headline, left aligned, two lines with no full stop, one word
written by hand with a brush underline in the frame's bot's colour (ink when it is no one bot's). The painted black bot of
the icon is on every frame.

**The first three are one idea each, and one panorama.** App Store search shows an app's first three portrait
screenshots side by side, tiny, and Google Play shows its first ones large; so each of the three says one thing in three
or four words of big type, with one big picture, and one clay bar (the icon's) runs across all three at the same height:
from it hang the bot, the X0 widget and a phone. After them come the app's screens as white cards: the top of a real
screen, one part lifted out with an arrow at it, and the bot peeking over the card's edge with a face for the frame.

Out of both sets, still defined in `frames.mjs`: notes (the meetings are noted on the computer), code (the first three
are Code bot's: its question on the widget, its job at work) and connect (other companies' marks; `off`). The widget, the
Live Activity and the keyboard are drawn from their own specs by `tools/media/store/surfaces.mjs` (they are drawn by the
phone itself, not by the screens' renderer); the bot under the bar is the launch's own art, drawn big by the brand's
`launch.mjs` code (`art.mjs` renderHang). The photographs in `tools/media/store/photos/` belong to the earlier, dark set
and are not used by this one.

The default set is global: international names and places (Maya, Sofia, Kenji, Amara; Lisbon, Porto). Two frames keep an
Indian moment on purpose: Ask (a Hinglish request) and Spaces (a family with Nani).
`node tools/media/store/build.mjs --set india` builds the India set (Aanya, Pune) into `out/india/` for Google Play's
English (India) and Hindi listings.

The App Store takes ten (its limit), Google Play eight. The feature graphic is the icon's bot hanging from his bar, the
mark "X0" and one line, "Your team of bots", on white, everything that reads well inside its middle (Play crops its
edges on some devices).

### App Preview

Built by `cd TAILZU-BACKEND/tulmi && npx tsx ../../TAILZU-FRONTEND/tools/media/store/preview.mts` into
`tools/media/store/out/preview/` (not in git: about 30 MB): `x0-app-preview.mp4`, 886 x 1920 (the 6.9" preview size),
H.264 High 4.0 at 10 Mbps, 30 fps, 24.3 s, with a silent stereo AAC track (App Store Connect wants an audio track), and
`poster.png`, its poster frame (set the poster to 00:06.4 in App Store Connect). Only the app: no hands, no device, no
price. The beats:

1. 0–2.2 s: the launch. The bot pulls himself up over his bar and is gone, the bar is pulled in, Home is there (the real
   launch art moved by `app/src/launch/launchCore.ts`'s own numbers).
2. 2.2–5 s: Home with the bots, Code bot asking.
3. 5–10 s: the Home Screen. The X0 widget asks the same question; "Per item" is taken in place (Done), and Code bot is
   back at work on "Fixing cart".
4. 10–17.6 s: the Lock Screen. Bots at work runs as a Live Activity (Build, Check, Ship), then "Done — tap to see the PR";
   a tap opens the app on the job: "Shipped. PR #128 is ready".
5. 17.6–22.8 s: Keybot. X0's family chat, "Dinner at 8, everyone home?"; the Peek-a-bot key listens, thinks, and
   "Running late, start without me." lands in the box, sent.

## Before you submit

- **Review account.** The app signs in with a one-time code. Make an account for review whose sign-in works without a
  live inbox, and put its details in App Store Connect (App Review Information) and Play Console (App access).
- **Account deletion on the phone.** Done: You › Your data › Delete account on the phone, and You, Privacy and Settings
  on the computer (`bots.delete-account`). The review notes point there.
- **Support URL.** It is https://x0bot.com/faq, which answers today (x0bot.com/support does not). Once the server is deployed, use
  https://x0bot.com/support instead (it has support@x0bot.com on it).
- **The icon.** The painted bot hanging under the clay bar (`tools/media/brand/x0-icon/curious-hang/8-painted-clay-bar`).
  The build carries it (`app/assets/icon.png`, and the Android adaptive layers), so the App Store takes it from the
  build. Upload these by hand, from `tools/media/store/out/icons/`:
  - Google Play › Main store listing › App icon: `play-512.png` (512 x 512).
  - Google Cloud › OAuth consent screen › App logo: `google-oauth-120.png` (120 x 120). Changing the logo may ask for
    the consent screen to be verified again; keep it in production while that runs.
  - `app-store-1024.png` is the App Store's 1024 icon, if a form asks for it.
